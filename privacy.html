Privacy Policy & Client Data Protection

Our operational commitments to customer data privacy, military-grade data sanitization, and local storage usage.

Data Stewardship Standards

1. Front-End Prototype Data Storage

This web application operates as a client-side prototype. Your shopping cart, saved wishlist, demo orders, sell quotes, and repair requests are stored locally on your device using HTML5 LocalStorage APIs (keys: bss_cart, bss_wishlist, bss_orders, bss_sell_requests, bss_repair_requests). No payment card data, passwords, or personal credentials are transmitted to remote cloud databases by this static front-end website.

2. Certified Pre-Owned Data Erasure (DoD 5220.22-M)

When you sell a laptop, desktop, SSD, or hard disk to Bheral Systems & Services, our laboratory adheres strictly to the Department of Defense (DoD 5220.22-M) and NIST 800-88 sanitization standards. Every storage sector undergoes a multi-pass binary overwrite cycle to prevent any forensic recovery of proprietary corporate files, emails, or personal photographs.

3. Contact Information Usage

Customer contact details (Name, Phone number, Delivery address, Delhi pincode) provided during checkout, sell booking, or repair scheduling are utilized solely to:

  • Coordinate technician arrival time and verify doorstep delivery eligibility.
  • Transmit real-time milestone updates and written diagnostic reports via SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Issue official GST-compliant tax invoices and warranty guarantee documentation.

4. Managing Your Local Storage

You retain full authority to delete all stored cart items, saved wishlists, and demo request IDs at any time by clearing your browser's site data or clicking 'Clear All Items' on the cart and wishlist pages.
