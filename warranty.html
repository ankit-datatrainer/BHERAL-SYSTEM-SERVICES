Warranty Coverage & Doorstep Service Policy

Complete clarity on what our 6-Month Replacement Warranty covers, claim procedures, and Delhi NCR pickup boundaries.

6-Month Written Guarantee

1. Refurbished Computer Warranty Scope

Every certified refurbished laptop and custom desktop system purchased from Bheral Systems & Services includes our documented 6-Month Written Replacement Warranty starting from the recorded delivery date.

What is Covered:

  • Motherboard & Power IC: Unprompted boot failures, sudden restarts, and board chip malfunctions.
  • Processor & Core Logic: Thermal throttling faults under standard workload.
  • Installed RAM Memory: Memory errors, BSOD memory management faults, and stick detection issues.
  • Solid State Drive (SSD): Storage read/write controller failure or unrecognised boot volumes.
  • Original Display Panel: Ghosting, sudden backlight failure, or line distortion not originating from physical impact.
  • Keyboard & Trackpad: Unresponsive keys or trackpad sensor failure.

What is Excluded:

  • Accidental physical damage, drops, broken hinges from violent lid force, or cracked LCD glass.
  • Liquid spillage, moisture corrosion, or chemical contamination.
  • Power surge damage caused by substandard non-earthed domestic electrical sockets or non-certified third-party chargers.
  • Unauthorized modifications, tampering with warranty void tamper-evident security seals, or BIOS chip reprogramming.
  • Normal consumable chemical battery wear over extended months (batteries are guaranteed to hold a minimum 80% baseline upon delivery).
